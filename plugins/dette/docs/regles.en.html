How does debt money work?

The bank (played by a player or the facilitator) creates money, granting it as credit that borrowers must later repay with interest. This is how most of today's monetary systems work in most contemporary economies.

What a facilitator needs to know

  • New credit: a player borrows an amount (the principal) from the bank, plus interest to repay later.
  • Repayment: a player repays all or part of their credit (interest only, or interest + principal).
  • Cannot pay: if a player can't honor their debt between turns, the bank seizes what they own - the assistant automatically classifies the outcome (simple seizure, prison, or bankruptcy) based on what could be recovered and how many cards remain.
  • Money mass: it only moves as credits are granted and repaid - unlike free money, it never naturally converges towards a target.

The card/money factor and the weak coin value can be adjusted when creating the game - they set the equivalence between physical value cards and money tokens.
