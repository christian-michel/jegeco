How does free money work?

Money is created symmetrically by each participant according to the rules of Stéphane Laborde's Relative Theory of Money (RTM): each player receives a regular Universal Dividend, with no debt or interest to repay. This is the principle behind June (Ğ1) money.

What a facilitator needs to know

  • No bank: no player nor the bank holds a special role - money appears directly with each player.
  • Universal Dividend (UD): at each death/rebirth, the player starts over with the current UD (shown by the assistant, no need to calculate it by hand). Other players see their money mass naturally converge towards the average over the turns.
  • One-token penalty (optional): the official rules call for removing one weak token on money rotation - can be enabled when creating the game.
  • Galileo module: on the end-of-game report, a dedicated chart shows accounts converging towards the average over time - the visual signature of free money.

To learn more about the RTM: trm.creationmonetaire.info.
