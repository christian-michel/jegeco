How does barter work?

An exchange system with no money and no bank: players directly exchange goods with each other, never going through an intermediary monetary unit.

What a facilitator needs to know

  • Only trades: every transaction must go both ways - never a one-sided gift, never any money or token of any kind.
  • No imposed value: unlike the other two systems, the game sets no fixed scale - everything is freely negotiated between players, case by case.
  • Sets of four still matter: gathering 4 identical cards to move up a level (weak → medium → strong) creates scarcity and fuels negotiation, but a card always counts as 1 in the final wealth calculation, regardless of its level.
  • Death/rebirth: just like the other two systems, mandatory each relevant turn, with an inventory of the dying player's goods before they're reborn with the starting allotment.

The "Trade between players" button, available mid-turn on the dashboard, records each transaction as it happens - unlike credits in debt money, there's no dedicated step in the end-of-turn assistant.
